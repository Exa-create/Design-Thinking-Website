the journey

Four prototypes, four lessons.

Each iteration documented with date, print time, material cost, and what we learned.

Prototype 1 — The "Oh, that's how thin 1mm is" moment

Fail

26 / 05 / 2026 · 12m 57s · 3.02g · $0.09

Wrong print orientation, walls too thin and snapped instantly. Board didn't fit. Pin slots too small. No ventilation. No lid. Beautiful disaster.

Read full breakdown →

Prototype 2 — Two-piece design, same-size mistake

Fail

01 / 06 / 2026 · 20m 01s · 4.25g · $0.13

Introduced a two-piece design with a leg-and-hole lid system. Made the legs and holes the same size — they didn't snap, they just slid. Button holes in wrong position. Board still misaligned. No ventilation.

Read full breakdown →

Prototype 3 — It... almost works.

Needs Improvement

02 / 06 / 2026 · 28m 49s · 6.19g · $0.19

Board fits correctly. Leg-and-hole lid system actually clips. Ventilation slots added. Pins accessible. One remaining issue: the lid's interior recess isn't deep enough — it can't close flush.

Read full breakdown →
