Project Overview

We are working on designing and 3D printing a protective case for the ESP32-C3 Zero microcontroller as part of our TAFE project. The case needs to fit the board correctly, protect it during classroom use, allow access to the USB-C port, pins and buttons, and include ventilation holes for airflow and cooling.


The main users of this product are IT students and teachers in a TAFE classroom and workshop environment. Students use the ESP32-C3 Zero during practical lessons where the board gets handled a lot, connected to computers via USB-C, and shared between team members. The case needs to be durable enough for everyday classroom use, allow easy access to the USB-C port, pins and buttons for programming and testing, and include ventilation so the board stays at a safe temperature.


Each team member created their own first prototype individually. We then came together as a team, compared what worked and what didn't, and used the best ideas from each design to guide the next iteration. This website documents Hasan Kandemir's individual design journey.
