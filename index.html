project overview

The goal — a snug, vented, snap-fit enclosure

A 3D-printed protective case for the ESP32-C3 Zero microcontroller. Must fit the board exactly, protect exposed GPIO pins and the USB-C port, provide ventilation for heat dissipation, and include a removable lid for board access.

Designed iteratively across four prototypes — each one teaching us what didn't work on the previous. The final design applies every lesson learned: corrected tolerances, working snap-fit lid, ventilation slots, and proper button alignment.
