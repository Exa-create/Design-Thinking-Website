What changed, what failed

Adding a lid — and learning about interference fits

The big leap for Prototype 2 was splitting the case into a base + lid design, joined by a leg-and-hole locking system. Conceptually solid — print a hole on the base, print a matching leg on the lid, push them together for a snap fit.

The problem? I made the legs and the holes the exact same size in CAD. In real life, that means the legs slide in but don't actually grip anything — there's no interference fit, no friction, no snap. The lid would just fall off. (For a proper snap fit, the legs need to be very slightly larger than the holes, or have a small barb / lip.)

On top of that, the button cut-outs were in the wrong position relative to the actual board's button layout, the board still sat misaligned inside the base, and there were still no ventilation openings anywhere.

Net result: better than P1 in concept, still unusable in practice.
