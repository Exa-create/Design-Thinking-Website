Most boxes ticked. One to go.

Full design brainstorm before this iteration paid off. Internal dimensions corrected against the physical board (not just datasheet) with proper FDM tolerance allowance. Board slides in snugly.

Leg-and-hole snap fit redesigned — legs slightly smaller than holes with friction-fit grip. Lid actually clips into place. Ventilation slots added to the sides. USB-C, GPIO, and buttons all accessible.

The one outstanding issue: the interior recess on the underside of the lid isn't deep enough — it presses against the top of the chip, so the case can't close flush. Small visible gap remains. Fix is straightforward: deepen the lid recess in the final design.

🟢 Pros
  • Board fits correctly — no forcing
  • Snap-fit lid clips properly
  • Ventilation slots functional
  • All ports + buttons accessible
  • Walls solid on handling
  • First almost-usable prototype
🔴 Remaining issues
  • Lid interior recess too shallow
  • Lid presses against top of chip
  • Case can't close completely flush
  • Small visible gap remains
🛠️ Planned for Final
  • Deepen lid interior recess to clear chip
  • Final tolerance audit before print
  • Verify lid sits flush on case body
  • Final ventilation pattern review
