Description

Prototype 3 showed a significant improvement over both previous prototypes. Following a full design brainstorm and review of a classmate's approach, the entire lid and closure system was rethought. The bottom half printed successfully with correct internal dimensions — the ESP32-C3 Zero sits securely with a small amount of clearance, providing a comfortable fit without being too loose. The GPIO pins protrude through the openings correctly with no obstruction. Ventilation slots were added to the sides of both the base and the lid, providing airflow and cooling for the board during use. The leg-and-hole connection system for the top half also worked correctly for the first time — the legs fitting into the holes smoothly without sticking or requiring force. The only remaining issue identified was that the interior recess of the top half was not hollowed out enough, causing the lid to press against the top of the chip and prevent the case from closing fully. This single fix is all that is required before the final design can be produced.

Changes from Prototype 2

→ What was changed and why

  • Full design brainstorm completed before starting CAD — informed better design decisions
  • Internal dimensions corrected — board now fits with comfortable clearance, no sanding
  • Lid interior recess redesigned — though still not deep enough, the concept is correct
  • Leg-and-hole system fixed — legs are now slightly smaller than holes so they connect smoothly
  • Ventilation slots added to sides of base and lid for airflow
  • Button cutout positioning reviewed and improved

Pros & Cons

✓ Pros

  • Board fits correctly with proper clearance — no sanding needed
  • GPIO pins protrude through slots with no obstruction
  • Leg-and-hole lid system works — fits smoothly without force
  • Ventilation slots present on sides for airflow
  • Most complete and functional prototype so far
  • USB-C port accessible through opening

✗ Cons / Remaining Issue

  • Interior recess of top half not hollowed out enough
  • Lid presses against top of chip — case cannot close fully
  • Small visible gap between top and bottom halves when assembled

Planned Improvements for Final Design

→ Changes to implement

  • Increase the depth of the interior recess in the top half so it clears the top of the ESP32-C3 Zero chip
  • Verify all other dimensions are correct before printing
  • Test closure — lid should sit flush against the base with no gap
  • Confirm ventilation slots, pin openings, USB-C cutout and button cutouts all pass testing criteria
