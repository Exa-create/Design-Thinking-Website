The one where most things finally worked

Most boxes ticked. One left to tick.

Prototype 3 is where the lessons from P1 and P2 paid off. The internal dimensions were finally measured against the physical board (not just the datasheet), with appropriate tolerance for FDM printer accuracy. The board slides in snugly without being forced.

The leg-and-hole snap fit was redesigned — legs slightly oversized relative to the holes, with a subtle barb. This time the lid actually clips into place. Ventilation slots were added across the top and side panels, allowing real airflow for the board during operation.

USB-C, GPIO pins, and onboard buttons are all accessible without removing the case. This is the first prototype that functions.

The one outstanding issue: the interior recess on the underside of the lid isn't deep enough. The lid sits proud of the case body — about 1mm off — and won't close completely flush. The fix for the final design is straightforward: deepen the lid's interior pocket so it can sink fully onto the case lip.
